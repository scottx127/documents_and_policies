Privacy Policy for Twenty0 Cricket

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Twenty0 Cricket (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a cricket drafting and simulation game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

Game data stored on your device

Twenty0 Cricket stores game progress locally on your device. This can include your selected eras, drafted team, match results, player statistics, settings, and challenge progress. This data is used to make the game work and is not uploaded by us unless you use online leaderboard features.

Leaderboard information

If you use online leaderboards, the app may use Firebase services to create an anonymous technical identifier and store leaderboard entries. A leaderboard entry may include:

  • Your chosen leaderboard display name;
  • Cricket player names associated with player records;
  • Record values, match or challenge statistics, and ranking information;
  • An anonymous Firebase user identifier used to identify your own records.

Advertising information

The app may display full-screen interstitial advertisements through Google AdMob at natural breaks in gameplay. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, app interaction information, and advertising data in order to provide, measure, limit, or personalise ads where permitted by law and your choices.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Save and restore your game progress;
  • Operate and display online leaderboards;
  • Prevent duplicate leaderboard submissions and maintain rankings;
  • Provide, measure, and improve advertising;
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the app and its online services.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Firebase for anonymous authentication and online leaderboards, and may use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. These providers process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Advertising choices and consent

Where required by applicable law, we will request consent before using personalised advertising. You may also be able to manage advertising preferences through your device settings.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. Leaderboard information is retained while needed to operate the leaderboard, unless you request deletion or we no longer need it.

To request deletion of your leaderboard display name and associated leaderboard entries, contact us at [YOUR PRIVACY EMAIL ADDRESS] and include the display name used in the app.

Children’s privacy

Twenty0 Cricket is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system is completely secure.

International processing

Firebase and AdMob are Google services and may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this page with a revised effective date.
