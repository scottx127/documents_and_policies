Idle Business Empire Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Idle Business Empire is an idle business simulation game published by X127. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information we collect

Idle Business Empire does not require an account. The game lets you enter a player name and business names. These are stored locally on your device as part of your game save and are not sent to our servers.

We do not ask for your email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, camera, or payment card information.

Your game progress, businesses, employees, bonuses, settings, and audio preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this gameplay information.

Advertising

Idle Business Empire may offer optional rewarded advertisements, for example to receive a random bonus or increase a reward. If advertising is enabled, the advertising provider may collect or use device and online identifiers, approximate location inferred from an IP address, advertising interaction information, and related technical information to deliver, measure, limit, and protect advertising.

Rewarded advertisements are optional. You can continue playing Idle Business Empire without watching an advertisement. We do not sell your personal information.

Where Google advertising services are used, Google’s privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy. You may manage advertising choices through your device settings and Google’s My Ad Center.

In-app purchases

Idle Business Empire may offer optional in-app purchases, such as a bonus booster pack. Purchases are processed by the relevant app store, such as Apple’s App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

How information is used

Local game data is used only to provide game features, including saving progress, calculating offline earnings, applying bonuses, restoring your game state, and keeping track of settings.

Advertising-related information, if collected by an advertising provider, is handled according to that provider’s privacy policy to provide and measure rewarded advertisements and help prevent fraud.

Children

Idle Business Empire is not directed at children under the age at which parental consent is required in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address the concern.

Data retention and deletion

Because game progress is stored locally, you can remove it by clearing Idle Business Empire app data in your device settings or uninstalling the app. Advertising providers retain any information they collect according to their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Idle Business Empire changes, including if we add services or features that change how information is handled. The updated version will be posted at this address with a revised “Last updated” date.
