Quick Broadcast – Account Deletion

You can delete your Quick Broadcast account and associated data directly from within the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Quick Broadcast.
  2. Open the menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Open Account Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the confirmation steps.

If you cannot access the app or need help, email us at: x127.scott@gmail.com.

What data is deleted

Deleting an account removes the account and its associated Quick Broadcast data, including:

  • Account and family-group information
  • Paired-device information and notification tokens
  • Broadcast messages and acknowledgement records
  • Quick-broadcast settings, widget shortcuts, and preferences stored for the account

If the deleted account owns a family group, its associated family data is also removed.

Data retention

Account deletion is processed as part of the deletion request in the app. Limited technical records may remain temporarily in secure service backups before being automatically removed under the retention practices of our service providers.
